## Placement and evidence

Place a selected symbol immediately after the complete claimed brand, preferably at the prominent or first substantive mention. Do not inject symbols into URLs, repository slugs, code identifiers, paths, or legal party names. Do not stack ™ and ℠.

™ signals a goods-mark claim and ℠ a service-mark claim; neither requires federal registration or proves clearance. ® is reserved for a verified registration and its covered goods/services. Emoji/text variation selectors do not create different legal statuses. Store risk, ownership, registration, and approval as separate explicit fields.

Copyright is independent of trademark. Existing work-specific ownership, copyright years, licenses, assignments, and third-party notices remain controlling. This display update does not change those records or create new rights.

Historical snapshots, completed records, quoted former names, and compatibility paths remain evidence. Older naming instructions are superseded for current display only. Keep functional architecture questions separate from this display cleanup.
